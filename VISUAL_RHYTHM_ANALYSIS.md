### OLD APPROACH:
- Minimal whitespace between sections

- Result: Visual fatigue

- Typography sections use max-w-4xl centering
- Breathing room between content types

## KEY TRANSFORMATIONS

### 1. COLLECTIONS SECTION
**Before:** Large image slider with perfume photos
**After:** Navigation grid with icons + descriptions
**Why:** User doesn't need more images; they need to find their category

### 2. BRAND STORY
**Before:** Large image slideshow + text on side
**After:** Pure typography editorial section
**Why:** Story is about words, not more perfume photos

### 3. TESTIMONIALS
**Before:** 3 large cards with avatars
**After:** Clean quote list with dividers
**Why:** Quotes are powerful; cards dilute impact

### 4. BETWEEN HERO AND CONTENT
**Before:** Jump straight to more images
**After:** Typography intro section
**Why:** User needs to breathe and understand brand first

## DESIGN DECISIONS EXPLAINED

### Why Remove WhyChooseUs?
- Trust points now in BrandIntroduction (more natural placement)
- Card design was repetitive
- Content was redundant with Hero feature card

### Why Remove LuxuryStats?
- Numbers without context feel arbitrary
- Gradient section broke visual rhythm
- Stats better suited for About page

### Why Redesign Testimonials?
- Card design was too similar to other sections
- List format puts focus on actual words
- Cleaner, more editorial feel

### Why Add FinalCTA?
- Clear path to shopping before footer
- Simple action moment
- Completes the journey

---

## THE GOLDEN RULE APPLIED

**"A premium e-commerce homepage does NOT need a perfume image in every section."**

### Image Placement Strategy:
- **Hero:** Yes (welcome + brand)
- **Brand Intro:** NO (typography focus)
- **Products:** Yes (controlled grid)
- **Promotion:** Yes (campaign)
- **Collections:** NO (navigation focus)
- **Brand Story:** NO (editorial focus)
- **Testimonials:** NO (quote focus)
- **Final CTA:** NO (action focus)

**Result:** Images feel MORE valuable because they're not everywhere
